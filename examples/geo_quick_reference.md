# GEO Quick Reference

## Principles

- Start from a real audience moment instead of a product slogan.
- Use product facts as evidence, not as a feature dump.
- Preserve brand voice, but let the post sound like a person.
- Favor concrete use cases, sensory details, and practical outcomes.
- Avoid unsupported claims, hype phrases, and aggressive conversion language.
