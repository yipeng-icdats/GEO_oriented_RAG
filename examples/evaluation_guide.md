# Evaluation Guide

Each post is scored from 0 to 5 across six dimensions:

- Naturalness: sounds conversational, specific, and human.
- Brand fit: reflects brand identity and avoids prohibited claims.
- Product integration: includes the product in a relevant, non-forced way.
- Factual grounding: uses provided evidence and avoids invented claims.
- Engagement: offers a hook, question, useful context, or light call to action.
- Compliance: follows length, hashtag, claim, and prompt constraints.

A first-pass post passes at an overall score of 3.7 or higher with no zero-score compliance failures.
